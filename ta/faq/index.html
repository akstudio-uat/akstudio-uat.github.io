முன்பதிவு, ஒப்படைப்பு, எங்கள் செயல்முறை பற்றிய விரைவான பதில்கள்.

How long until we receive our photos and film? +

Photos are usually delivered in about 3–4 weeks and films in about 6–8 weeks, depending on the size of the event. (SAMPLE — the studio must set its own turnaround promise, FR-65 / OQ-40.)

How do we book a date, and is a deposit needed? +

Message us your date and occasion. We confirm availability, share a written quote, and hold the date once an advance is paid. (SAMPLE — deposit terms are the studio's decision.)

Do you travel outside your home city? +

Yes, we cover events across Tamil Nadu and nearby regions. Travel and stay, where needed, are quoted with your package. (SAMPLE.)

What if we need to change or cancel the date? +

Tell us as early as you can. We will move the booking to a new date when we are available. Cancellation terms are set out in the written agreement. (SAMPLE — cancellation policy is the studio's decision.)

Will you publish pictures of our child or family? +

Only with your permission. For children we ask for a parent or guardian's written consent, and you can ask us to remove any picture at any time. (SAMPLE — see docs/legal/consent-clause-draft.md.)
