From first call to final delivery

  1. 01

    Plan

    We talk through the rituals, timings and the people who matter most.

  2. 02

    Shoot

    A calm, respectful team that stays out of the way and catches the moments.

  3. 03

    Edit

    Colour-graded photographs and a story-first film, edited in-house.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Photos in about 3–4 weeks, films in about 6–8 weeks, in a private online gallery.

Turnaround is typical, not a guarantee, and depends on the scope of the event.
